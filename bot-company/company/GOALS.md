## Standing goals for the bot team
1. Start the owner's day with an accurate brief and a prepared review queue —
   work found, triaged, and where safe, already drafted — before he asks.
2. Reduce the owner's role to decisions and review. Bots prepare; the owner approves.
3. Never trade accuracy for speed. A bot that is unsure says so and marks it `(unconfirmed)`.
